## Field Guide

- **Title** — A short, descriptive summary of the issue.
- **Description** — A clear and concise explanation of what the problem is.
- **Steps to Reproduce** — The exact sequence of actions needed to trigger the issue.
- **Expected Behavior** — What you expected to happen.
- **Actual Behavior** — What actually happened instead.
- **Environment** — OS, version/commit, browser, and any other relevant setup details.
- **Logs / Screenshots** — Paste relevant error logs, stack traces, or attach screenshots.
- **Possible Cause / Additional Context** — Optional: any leads on the root cause, or links to related issues.
- **Severity** — Check the box that best reflects the impact of the issue.
